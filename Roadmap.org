Here are some excerpts from a conversation I had with an LLM:

In org mode, file links can be done with `::` followed by the line number:

[[file:/home/peter/Dropbox-dec/blog/blog/gng.rkt::42][open at line 42]]

You can also use a relative path:

[[file:./file.txt::10][open file.txt at line 10]]

When you follow the link, Emacs opens the file and moves point to the specified line. You can also use a search string instead of a line number if that is more robust:

[[file:/path/to/file::text to find][open at matching text]]


Ok, what about linking to buffers?
Org does not have a dedicated `buffer:` link type, but you can use an `elisp:` link:

[[elisp:(switch-to-buffer "*Messages*")][open the *Messages* buffer]]

To jump to a particular line, combine `switch-to-buffer` with `goto-char` and `forward-line`:

[[elisp:(progn (switch-to-buffer "my-buffer") (goto-char (point-min)) (forward-line 41))][open my-buffer at line 42]]

Be aware that following `elisp:` links may require confirmation, depending on your Org security settings.
